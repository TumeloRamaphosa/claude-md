## 3. Hook Taxonomy (first 3 seconds of each top video)

Derived from `0:00` chapter titles, lead sentences, and titles.

| Hook type | % of top 20 | When it works | Example |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **STATEMENT** (declarative, no question) | 65 % | Tool launches, framework releases, "next evolution" trend calls | "Google Just Dropped a Masterclass…" / "Vercel Eve changes production AI agents" |
| **CONTRARIAN / love-then-pivot** | 15 % | Audience already knows the topic — pivot earns the click | "I love Karpathy's wiki, but it doesn't scale" / "Best AI setup isn't most autonomous" |
| **QUESTION / insider term** | 5 % | Definitions, jargon videos for newcomers | "What separates top agentic engineers?" |
| **SURPRISE / curiosity gap** | 5 % | Insider behavior, "they actually do X?!" | "Claude Code creators don't prompt their agents" |
| **STORY / build-in-public arc** | 5 % | Long-running experiments | "I'm building an AI Dark Factory" |
| **DEMO (target video)** | 5 % | Hardware/hands-on, behind-paywall | "I wired OpenCode to a 4× Mac Studio cluster" |

### The 0:00 rule (key insight)
- **None** of the top 20 lead with a literal "How to" or "Tutorial" — the word "FULL Guide" only appears once, and it's the 67-min monster.
- Cole's 0:00 always opens with a **completed thought**, not a question: "Google just dropped…", "Vercel Eve changes…", "The new best way is…".
- The video title + first chapter title together do all the SEO/curiosity lifting; the on-camera hook is a **declarative statement** in a normal tone, often without the thumbnail's hype.
- When the topic is opinion-based, he **flips the conventional wisdom** ("love it BUT here's what works better") rather than asking a question.

### Hook → topic fit (for our channels)
| Topic type | Recommended hook |
|---|---|
| New product / partnership announcement | STATEMENT (e.g., "StudEx just signed with X hotel in Russia") |
| Behind-the-scenes process reveal | DEMO (e.g., "I watched Naledi negotiate 14 vendors in 90 minutes") |
| Industry myth-busting | CONTRARIAN-LOVE |
| Founder update / milestone | STORY / build-in-public |
| Tool launch (e.g., new biltong SKU, AI agent) | STATEMENT with mechanism (analogous to "Vercel Eve changes X") |

## 4. Thumbnail Formula

Based on vision-model analysis of all 20 maxresdefault thumbnails + Cole's overall channel.

### 4.1 Color palette (consistent across the channel)
| Element | Color | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Background base | **Dark navy → black** (`#0a0f24` to `#000`) | 18/20 thumbs |
| Primary accent | **Bright cyan / electric blue** (`#3aa0ff`–`#5ad1ff`) | Headlines, glows, outlines |
| Secondary accent | **Magenta / hot pink** (`#e64fa9`) | Logos, focal objects |
| Tertiary accent | **Google-brand multicolor / orange** (`#ff7a1a`) | Sparingly, to call out a competitor logo |
| Highlight | **Pure white** | Title text, key word overlays |
| Skin tones | Warm cinematic key light on the right side | Face is always lit from front-right |

### 4.2 Composition grid (rule of thirds, NOT centered)
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ TITLE LARGE TOP-RIGHT  (60 % W) │
│                                 │
│        [center glow/            │
│         metaphor object]        │
│                                 │
│ [FACE L 40 %]      [DATA RIGHT] │
│                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```
- **Face presence:** 18/20 have Cole's face, positioned **left ~40 %** of frame, cropped chest-up, looking at camera with calm/slight-smirk expression (not screaming).
- **Right side** carries the **concept artifact**: iceberg, folder tree, monitor array, glowing logo, card stack.
- **Title** sits in the **upper-right or upper-center**, never overlapping the face.

### 4.3 Text overlay style
- **Font:** heavy sans-serif (custom or Montserrat/Inter Bold), slightly italicized on occasion.
- **Hierarchy:**
  - One **2-3 word headline** in giant bold, often **2 lines** (e.g., "GOOGLE'S / AI PLAYBOOK", "BEAUTIFUL / FRONTENDS", "AI CODING / 10x").
  - A **brand label or sub-line** above in smaller text, often in the brand's official colors (Google's G/Y/R/B/G/R, Anthropic's orange, Vercel's triangle).
- **Treatment:** plain text, no stroke, no drop shadow — relies on **dark bg contrast** to pop.
- **Avoid:** all-caps "SHOCKING", "OMG", red arrows, yellow highlights (Cole never uses these).

### 4.4 Visual hooks (recurring devices)
- **Metaphor objects** that embody the concept: iceberg, neon staircase, glowing folder, city skyline, pixel-art invader, slime blob.
- **Glow / neon outlines** on logos and key shapes (cyan/magenta gradient).
- **Arrows converging on a central element** when summarizing many tools (Omnigent thumbnail).
- **Brand logo wall** for tool-launch videos (every relevant vendor logo appears).
- **Pixel/retro gaming** when the topic is meta/cultural critique (AI YouTube critique, full guide).

### 4.5 Outliers (what to avoid copying)
- **2 of 20** are pure-object, no-face thumbnails (the AI Marketing Factory and Pydantic 2.0). Both **underperformed** relative to face-present thumbs at the same topic tier. Cole's brand leverages his face heavily.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **StudEx Meat** (biltong, halal, SA-Russia) | Warm charcoal / smoky brown | Saffron gold + halal-green | Tumelo / founders | Bold sans, slightly industrial |
