## QUALITY STANDARDS

### Meat Grading Certification
```
AAA GRADE (Premium)
├─ Age: 6–8 months
├─ Weight: 14–18 kg
├─ Marbling: Abundant fat distribution
├─ Color: Bright red (fresh)
├─ Texture: Fine grain
├─ Cleanliness: All interior/exterior cleaned
└─ Target: 95%+ of orders

A GRADE (Standard)
├─ Age: 8–12 months
├─ Weight: 18–24 kg
├─ Marbling: Moderate
├─ Cleanliness: Full clean
└─ Acceptable: 3–5% of orders (discount 5–10%)

B GRADE (Economy)
├─ Age: 12+ months
├─ Lower marbling
├─ Acceptable: <2% (discount 15–20%, or reject)

GRADING PROTOCOL
├─ Seller pre-grades (responsibility)
├─ Mpho spot-check (random sample 20%)
├─ STUDEX QA verification (pre-shipment)
└─ Buyer final inspection (delivery)
```

### Rejection Criteria
```
AUTOMATIC REJECTION:
├─ Wrong animal type (not Ankole/specified breed)
├─ Grade below B (age >12 months, poor marbling)
├─ Health issues (skin lesions, odor)
├─ Improper cleaning (internal contaminants)
├─ Temperature excursion (thaw-refreeze signs)
└─ Regulatory non-compliance (missing tags, permits)

CONSEQUENCE:
├─ Seller reimburses Studex full cost
├─ Seller gets written warning
├─ 3 rejections → blacklist for 90 days
└─ Buyer gets full refund or re-shipment
```

## DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROTOCOL

### Level 1: Negotiated Settlement (7 days)
```
Issue raised by buyer/seller
  ↓
Mpho investigates (documentation review)
  ↓
Both parties discuss (call with Mpho)
  ↓
Agree on resolution:
  ├─ Quality refund (partial or full)
  ├─ Re-shipment
  ├─ Price adjustment
  └─ No action (if unsubstantiated)
```

### Level 2: Council Mediation (14 days)
```
If negotiation fails
  ↓
Deputy Chair + CFO_MEAT review evidence
  ↓
Recommend resolution to Chair
  ↓
Chair decides (binding)
  ├─ Refund to buyer
  ├─ Penalty to seller
  ├─ Insurance claim
  └─ Arbitration referral (if needed)
```

### Level 3: Arbitration (30 days)
```
If council decision rejected
  ↓
Refer to ICC (International Chamber of Commerce)
  ├─ Buyer lodges claim
  ├─ Seller responds
  ├─ Arbitrator hears evidence
  └─ Binding decision issued

ARBITRATION COST:
├─ Claimant pays initial filing fee ($2K–$5K)
├─ Loser pays arbitrator + other costs
└─ Studex Group holds dispute reserve (2% of monthly revenue)
```

### Example Disputes (Resolved)
```
DISPUTE 1: Quality complaint (low marbling)
├─ Buyer: Claims grade A delivered instead of AAA
├─ Mpho investigation: Spot-check found 15% sub-grade
├─ Resolution: Seller reimburses $5K (10% of order)
├─ Outcome: Seller gets 60-day probation, retrain QA
└─ Time to resolution: 5 days

DISPUTE 2: Delayed delivery (24 hours late)
├─ Buyer: Lost time-sensitive market window
├─ Mpho investigation: Freight forwarder delay (port congestion)
├─ Resolution: Studex refunds $2K (freight insurance)
├─ Outcome: Logistics partner reprimanded, alternate route planned
└─ Time to resolution: 3 days

DISPUTE 3: Payment default
├─ Seller: Buyer non-payment after 60 days
├─ Mpho investigation: Buyer's bank transfer failed (banking error)
├─ Resolution: Buyer re-submits payment, Studex absorbs 2% fee
├─ Outcome: Buyer goes on deposit pre-payment for 6 months
└─ Time to resolution: 10 days
```
