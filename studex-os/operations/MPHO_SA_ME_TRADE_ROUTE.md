VOLUME: 2–3 deals per week target = 500–1000 tons/month
MARGIN: 10–15% (Studex take) = $50K–$75K/month

## COMPLIANCE & RISK MANAGEMENT

### Export Requirements
```
SA SAPS (Sanitary & Phytosanitary)
├─ Health certificate
├─ Animal identification (ear tags)
├─ Farm inspection (30 days prior)
└─ Movement permit

ME IMPORT REQUIREMENTS (varies by country)
├─ Health certificate acceptance
├─ Customs pre-clearance
├─ Religious/Halal certification (if required)
└─ Labeling in local language
```

### Regulatory Compliance Checklist
```
□ OFAC screening (no sanctioned entities)
□ EU/HMT screening (no embargoes)
□ DAFF export permit
□ SAPS health certificate
□ Insurance certificate (CIF only)
□ Certificate of origin
□ Chamber of commerce registration
□ Port authority pre-clearance
□ End-use declaration (buyer signs)
```

### Dispute Resolution
```
Payment disputes:
├─ Buyer claims quality issue → Mpho verifies documentation
├─ Refund decision → STUDEX CFO + Buyer negotiation
└─ Arbitration (if unresolved) → ICC rules

Logistics disputes:
├─ Shipment delay → Freight forwarder negotiation
├─ Temperature excursion → Insurance claim
└─ Port damage → Customs inspector report

Regulatory disputes:
├─ Import denial → Mpho coordinates re-export or re-documentation
└─ Compliance issue → Department Ag escalation
```
