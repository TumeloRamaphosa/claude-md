## WHAT IS STUDEX OS?

A **distributed virtual machine** powering all Studex Group businesses through autonomous AI agents executing daily routines, coordinated by a central Super Brain intelligence layer.

**8 Business Nodes:**
- Studex Meat (premium beef trade)
- Studex Coffee (direct trade, D2C)
- Studex Global Markets (B2B commodities)
- Studex Gaming (tournaments + esports)
- Studex Arcade (gaming venues)
- Studex Agents (service delivery)
- Studex Droid (hardware + robotics)
- Studex Medical (healthcare)

**Agent Structure:** 3 autonomous agents per business (CMO, CFO, CTO) + Super Brain (cross-company intelligence)

**Daily Execution:** 6 checkpoint framework (06:00–21:00 SAST) with weekly revenue targets of $682K

## CORE CONCEPTS

### Business Ghost (Institutional Memory)
- **What:** Obsidian + ChromaDB + RAG queries
- **Stores:** Strategy, customers, finances, meetings, suppliers, competitors
- **Access:** Every agent queries via `/brain` command
- **Update:** Real-time after agent actions

### Agent OS (Execution Layer)
- **What:** 3 agents per business (CMO, CFO, CTO)
- **Daily:** 6 checkpoints (data → execute → report)
- **Coordination:** Discord commands + n8n workflows
- **KPIs:** Weekly revenue targets per agent

### Super Brain (Cross-Company Intelligence)
- **What:** Central Claude + custom models digesting 8 business reports
- **Daily:** 20:00 SAST synthesis (revenue, bottlenecks, risks, opportunities)
- **Output:** CEO briefing + strategic recommendations
- **Archive:** Long-term learning in Obsidian
