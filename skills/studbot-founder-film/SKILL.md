# studbot-founder-film

Use the approved rounded black-and-gold Studbot, amber eyes, glowing antenna and S emblem. Treat the founder's account as attributed personal history; distinguish it from future business plans. Studbot speaks about the founder unless first-person founder narration is requested. Do not clone a person's voice from written biography.

Read docs/studbot/FOUNDER-FILM.md and the supplied character reference before writing prompts. Never upload credential guides or confidential source decks as visual references. Maintain one canonical script and stable scene IDs. Keep identity references constant; preserve recorded model settings and actual job IDs.

Use Higgsfield's available model schema and upload tools, then generate the requested scenes. For batch submissions reconcile each entry: accepted jobs remain accepted even if siblings fail. A daily/account limit is a stop for new submissions, not permission to switch accounts or replay the batch. Check existing jobs before resuming.

Assemble with HyperFrames or Remotion when appropriate to the project, or the connected Higgsfield editing workflow. Choose one renderer. Probe actual duration/audio, inspect rendered frames, transcribe narration and compare with the script. Report uninspected aspects. Keep a local master and editable sources. Social publication requires a publishing request; video creation alone does not grant it.
